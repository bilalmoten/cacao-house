# Cacao House · Beyond the First Bar — expansion release

Prepared 4 October 2026. Product v1.1; save format 5. Existing Site identity and browser-storage keys are preserved. The original published version and the unpublished call-feedback checkpoint remain recoverable in Git and Library.

## Playable scope

The 16-week opening charter now leads to a second chapter reviewed after week 36, followed by open-ended play. An incomplete expansion review moves four weeks later without closing the business. The original opening loan/charter deadline remains a real loss condition.

- Saved production, per-product ingredient grades/fallback/allocation priority, factory assignment and pricing.
- Automatic per-ingredient purchasing rules from week 6, including supplier grade, incoming shipments, lead time and a protected cash reserve. Forecast includes next-week scheduled purchases and standing-order deposits. Manual repeat-order presets remain separate.
- Separate People, Facilities and Brand areas. Three owned sites: San Francisco Workshop, Oakland Factory, Turin Works. Two-week commissioning, machine versus staffed capacity, pay tiers, quality leads, expansion and mothballing have real costs and production effects.
- Standing café and department-store buyers from week 20: automatic recurring orders, product choice, three payment terms, immutable accepted terms, trust, escalating volumes, deadline fees, cooldown and recovery. Partnerships open Paris and Turin distribution; each market has its own customer fit and price multiplier.
- Persistent advertising budgets, lasting brand, scheduled competitor pressure and measurable price/service/brand responses.
- Four purposeful real destinations: Paris buyer brief/finishing; Turin hazelnuts/gianduja; Guayaquil cacao/direct trade; Kyoto and nearby Uji tea. Original fictional commercial partners. Travel consumes trading weeks while business controls remain available.
- Two permanent recipe variants, eight-case ingredient-consuming pilots at home, an actual Paris tasting order and delivery reward. Travel unlocks remote sourcing and a third factory connection.
- Original world, models, character art and animation retained. No new overseas 3D scenes; travel uses a schematic route network. No backend, accounts, payments, paid services or outsourcing.

## Verified

`npm run typecheck`, `npm test` and `npm run build` pass. Forty deterministic tests include forty complete opening campaigns, failed-action atomicity, cash and ingredient conservation, contract deadlines, delayed cash, bankruptcy/victory, save validation/migration, grade allocation and repeat-click safeguards. New scenarios cover:

- Actual v2 week-17 save plus representative v3/v4 migration to v5; all expanded state round-trips.
- Commissioning/payroll timing, unaffordable acquisition/hiring, mothballing and shared inventory.
- Eight ordinary weeks using unchanged production and automatic purchasing.
- Two factories plus an ingredient shortage, contract reservation and demand campaign; 185 produced cases reconcile to 30 contract and 155 retail cases.
- Buyer accepted-term immutability, missed-order cooldown, restored trust and delayed collections.
- Strong expansion path wins with two factories/four markets/two reliable buyers; slower one-factory path remains solvent and defers its review.
- Complete international chain, exact eight-case pilot consumption, arrival timing, one-time rewards, Paris fulfillment, tea production and Turin factory access.
- Chapter continuation resumes purchasing and standing orders exactly once. This regression was found during browser play and fixed before publication.

Browser QA on macOS Chromium used actual UI actions, not injected engine calls: imported the real completed opening save, set purchasing rules, acquired Oakland, hired staff, waited for commissioning, assigned a product, enabled two standing buyers and advertising, visited all four destinations, completed both pilots, delivered the Paris tasting, won the expansion and continued. The two buyers each achieved six successful deliveries, zero misses and trust 100. Week-38 exported save has $28,226.59 cash. The production bundle imported this save and reached week 39 with $27,849.04 cash, then reloaded successfully.

Eight decision panels checked at widths 320, 390, 768 and 1440 with no horizontal document or panel overflow. A mobile touch context verified navigation and a persistent staffing change. Production screenshots and the real UI-exported save are under `docs/qa/expansion/`. Browser scripts are preserved there. No page exceptions occurred in the expansion or production-bundle runs.

## Limits and release notes

Physical iPhone Safari and actual mobile thermal/performance behaviour have not been tested. Phone checks use Chromium viewport/touch emulation. Saves are local to each browser; export/import transfers between devices. No cloud synchronization. The JavaScript bundle includes Three.js and emits Vite's size advisory (about 316 kB gzip); build succeeds. Established growth can become comfortably profitable: this is a bounded playable campaign, not a claim of exhaustive long-term economic balance. Finite story content gives way to sandbox continuation.

Older historical report strings in migrated saves retain their original place names; current interface/content uses real cities. Stable internal IDs and the existing URL slug intentionally remain unchanged to preserve saves and links.
